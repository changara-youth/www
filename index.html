A group built on brotherhood and purpose

We are a youth-led Muslim group in Changara. We believe a young person who is grounded in faith, skilled in a trade and active in the community can change a whole neighbourhood.

Our members come together to learn, to save, to serve and to encourage each other. We work alongside families, elders and neighbours across Changara and Busia County, because lasting change is built together.

Faith and guidance

We share Islamic knowledge, good character and the habit of doing right in everyday life.

Youth empowerment

We open doors to skills, enterprise and peer support so members can stand on their own feet.

Community service

We take part in welfare, charity and local programmes that help our neighbours.

What we do

Three areas of work guide our programmes through the year.

Islamic education and reminders

Learning that shapes character, delivered in a friendly circle where every member can ask questions.

  • Halaqas and study circles
  • Weekly Jummah reminders
  • Guidance on Islamic ethics and daily conduct

Skills and economic growth

Practical support to help young people earn, save and start small ventures.

  • Group savings and shared investment
  • Entrepreneurship awareness sessions
  • Vocational skills and mentorship

Welfare and outreach

Caring for people around us and building peaceful relations across Changara.

  • Visiting and supporting the sick
  • Charity and community programmes
  • Peaceful coexistence and dialogue with neighbours
